Size ladder — where the full mark gives way to the compact one

Below roughly 40 px the three-step trail collapses into noise, which is the weakness I flagged. The compact variant drops to one satellite and a larger core, so the idea survives at favicon size. Rule: full mark above 40 px, compact below.
