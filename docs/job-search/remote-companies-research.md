### General advice for Kunal

1. **Apply on company careers portals** — Almost none of these accept cold-email résumés. LinkedIn "Easy Apply" often isn't the primary pipeline; always complete the official application.

2. **LinkedIn strategy** — Use Easy Apply for discovery, but **click through to the Greenhouse/Ashby/Lever posting** and apply there. Set job alerts for: "web designer," "website designer," "frontend," "UI designer," "customer success engineer," filtered by **Remote + India**.

3. **Referrals** — Highest ROI for India-remote roles at: **ElevenLabs, GitLab, Atlassian, Twilio, Canonical**. Search LinkedIn for "Talent Acquisition" or engineers at target companies; a warm referral beats Easy Apply.

4. **Portfolio positioning** — Lead with **AI-assisted web design** (Cursor/Claude), marketing sites, and Figma → code workflow. Many "Product Designer" roles here need 5–7+ years; **Website Designer / Web Designer / Frontend** titles are more realistic entry points.

5. **Timezone honesty** — Canonical (EMEA), US-sync roles (Goodwork-style fintech), and global async teams each expect different overlap. State IST availability and US/EU overlap hours in cover letters.

6. **Support as a bridge** — GitLab Customer Success Engineer, Yuno Technical Support Analyst, MongoDB Associate TSE — viable with B.Tech CS while building design portfolio internally.

7. **Verify before applying** — Job counts from the LinkedIn feed are likely stale. Confirm **location eligibility** on each posting; many say "Remote" but mean "Remote US" in fine print.

8. **Scam awareness** — StackAdapt publishes **careers@stackadapt.com** for fraud reporting. Twilio warns against WhatsApp/Telegram recruiters. Never pay for applications.
