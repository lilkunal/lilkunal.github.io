Lab

Skeletal animation, on request

A rigged character with fourteen clips: keyframe playback, crossfade blending between idle, walk and run, one-shot moves that return to where they left, and morph-target expressions. Built with three.js.

Nothing on this page downloads until you press the button. That is the point of it being here and not on every page: three.js and the model together are about 850 KB, and a portfolio that spends that on every visit is a portfolio that loses the visitor.

What is actually happening

Crossfade blending
Switching between idle, walk and run does not swap clips. Both play at once while their weights interpolate over 0.4s, so the skeleton never pops between poses.
One-shot with return
Jump, wave and punch play once, clamp on their final frame, then fade back to whichever clip was running before. The listener removes itself so repeated presses do not stack handlers.
Morph targets
Expressions are vertex blend shapes on the head mesh, driven by influence weights, not separate models.
Budget
The renderer stops when the canvas scrolls out of view or the tab is hidden. An animation loop that nobody is watching is just battery.

Techniques from the three.js examples for keyframe animation, skinning blending, multiple animations and morph targets, rebuilt on one model instead of four so the page costs 454 KB rather than 9 MB.
