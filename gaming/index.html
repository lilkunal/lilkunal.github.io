How I got here

roughly in order

  1. Where it started

    Terminator 2, 8-bit

    The grey console, two hard-edged controllers and a light gun you had to hold close to the TV. Mario, Contra and whatever else was on the cartridge that week. Everything since has been a bigger version of that.

  2. The CD-drive years

    GTA, Prince of Persia, Call of Duty, Project IGI

    The whole PC era on one hand-me-down machine. Prince of Persia I finished every part of, not just the famous one. Finished, not just installed.

  3. Between classes

    The flash game era

    Browser tabs full of games that loaded in twenty seconds and ate an afternoon. Half of what I know about how an interface should feel came from there.

  4. Now

    Valorant, most nights

    Five people, one plan, and about thirty seconds to change it. It sharpened how fast I read a screen, which turns out to be the same muscle a layout needs.

  5. Whenever the desk is not an option

    Mobile

    Queue, commute, waiting room. Different screen, same habit.
