Why work with me

I've been the customer on the other end of a bad website.

I've spent years in technical support — Teleperformance, Webhelp, Concentrix — talking to people whose systems weren't working. Alongside that, I run a manufacturing business that lives or dies by whether customers can find, understand and trust what's on offer, and nothing keeps you humble like customers — every single one of them. When I told people I was walking away from steady work to build this instead, plenty asked why. I knew what I was capable of; that part never wavered.

That combination means I don't design sites just to look good in a screenshot. I build them to load fast, explain the business clearly, and hold up long after launch — because I know exactly what it's like to be the one who answers for it when something breaks. None of that came from one mentor or one big break, either — I'm mostly the sum of everyone I've met and everyone who's travelled with me this far.
