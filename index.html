About

From the support desk to the web

I started on a Terminator 2 8-bit console and never really stopped pressing buttons. B.Tech in Computer Science from Aligarh College of Engineering & Technology, 2020.

Then four years on the other side of the screen: content moderation for Google platforms at Concentrix, customer support at Webhelp, technical support at Teleperformance. That is where I learnt how people actually get stuck, and it is still how I test everything I ship.

Since January 2025 I build for the web, from Gurugram, working remote: the Padma Lights catalogue of 200+ products selling in eight currencies, the internal ERP that tracks what every customer owes, and sites for small businesses.
