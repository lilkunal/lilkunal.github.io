Why work with me

I've been the customer on the other end of a bad website.

I've spent years in technical support — Teleperformance, Webhelp, Concentrix — talking to people whose systems weren't working. Alongside that, I handle marketing and the online side of Padma Enterprises, our family's manufacturing business — one that lives or dies by whether customers can find, understand and trust what's on offer online, and nothing keeps you humble like customers — every single one of them. When I told people I was walking away from steady work to build this instead, plenty asked why. I knew what I was capable of; that part never wavered.

That combination means I don't design sites just to look good in a screenshot. I build them to load fast, explain the business clearly, and hold up long after launch — because I know exactly what it's like to be the one who answers for it when something breaks. None of that came from one mentor or one big break, either — I'm mostly the sum of everyone I've met and everyone who's travelled with me this far.

How this works

Four steps, no jargon.

  1. 01

    We talk

    A call or WhatsApp message. You tell me what you sell, who buys it, and what isn't working right now. Free, and there's no obligation after it.

  2. 02

    You get a price in writing

    What I'll build, what it costs, and how long it takes — agreed before I start. The number doesn't move unless you ask for more.

  3. 03

    You see it before anyone else

    I send you a real link you can click and try on your own phone. You tell me what to change, and I change it.

  4. 04

    It goes live — and I stay reachable

    Your site goes up, and my number still works afterwards. Four years in technical support taught me that launch day isn't the finish line.

What I need from you

Your logo if you have one, photos of what you sell, and the details you want customers to see — address, hours, phone. No logo or photos? Say so and we'll work it out; that's a normal starting point, not a problem.
