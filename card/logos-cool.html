Six cool ones — these move

each still reads as a static mark when frozen for print · no initials

1 · Assembles itself

three slabs stack up, bottom to top, forever

Isometric and dimensional, so it has weight on a dark card. Frozen it is a clean stack; in a browser it builds itself, which is the thing you are selling.

2 · The scan

a light bar sweeps the page, like a crawler reading it

A page being indexed, drawn as a scanner passing over it. Sharp, a bit sci-fi, and it is exactly what Google does to a site you have just built.

3 · Pixel pull

loose pixels snap in toward one solid block

Scattered material being pulled into one finished thing. It also ties back to the pixel art already on your site, so your logo and your work look related.

Kunal Varshneyweb · seo · tools

4 · In orbit

traffic circling the page, always moving

A page with something orbiting it. Reads as reach and attention rather than a static document, and the single warm dot against the violet gives it a spark.

5 · Shipping

a progress bar filling inside a terminal window

The least decorative and the most honest: a build running to completion. Technical buyers will like it; it is the one mark here that looks like software rather than marketing.

6 · Draws itself

the growth line is drawn stroke by stroke, on loop

Dark tile, hot gradient line. The drawing animation is the whole personality: it looks like the result arriving rather than sitting there already finished.
