Daftar — The Office · دفتر

A civic web product that makes government work simpler for people across India’s 36 States & UTs — plain language, place-first navigation, and multilingual framing (English / हिंदी / اردو).

Status: Public demo on GitHub Pages — not a production government launch. Features and content are still evolving.

Problem

Government information is scattered, jargon-heavy, and hard to start from “where I live.” People need a calm entry point — find my place — before forms and portals.

  • Find my place + pick on map
  • Stats for States & UTs coverage

Current state

  • Clickable demo UX on GitHub Pages

What Daftar is about

Daftar (दफ़्तर / دفتر) means “the office” — the desk where official work happens. The product idea is to treat civic tasks like something a person can approach without already knowing which department owns what.

The demo is aimed at people who need Aadhaar, ration, police, land, school, or a State website — and need the right door in plain words. Instead of dumping a directory of schemes and PDFs, it leads with orientation: where are you? Find my place, pick on a map of India, or browse States & UTs. From there the interface can narrow what matters for that place.

Product surface in the demo

  • Find my place — search / entry to locate your State or UT
  • Pick on map — visual selection for people who think geographically
  • Coverage stats — at-a-glance numbers (36 States & UTs and related counts in the UI)
  • Language & theme — accessibility and preference without burying controls
  • Multilingual identity — English, Devanagari, and Urdu wordmarks so the brand reads as Bharat-facing, not English-only

My role

Concept, information architecture, visual design, and front-end build. I own the civic framing, the place-first UX, and the multilingual presentation. Iteration is AI-assisted (Claude / Cursor) with human review on clarity, mobile behaviour, and whether the copy still sounds like a person talking — not a portal template.
